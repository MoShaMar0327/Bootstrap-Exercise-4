About the Founder

Hi, I'm Mogamat Shareef Martin — outdoors enthusiast, builder, and founder of Lumber Jack's.

I started Lumber Jack's in 2026 after getting tired of overpriced outdoor equipment that fell apart after a single weekend trip. My goal is simple: curate durable, practical, and affordable gear that you can rely on in any environment.

Every item in our catalog is hand-picked for everyday utility and long-term toughness—whether you're camping in the wilderness or heading into the mountains.
